The Itinerary

Day One

The Drive Begins

Wednesday · July 1

  • 8:00 AM

    Depart Newport Beach

    Leave early — the drive is ~7 hours with stops. Pack snacks and a good playlist.

  • 10:30 AM

    Santa Barbara — Lunch Stop

    Stroll Stearns Wharf, grab lunch (see food recommendations below). Don't linger too long — Big Sur is waiting.

  • 1:00 PM

    Continue North on Hwy 1

    The famous Big Sur coastline opens up past San Luis Obispo. Windows down. Take your time.

  • 5:00 PM

    Check in · Big Sur Lodge

    Inside Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park. Your room key also gets you into 3 nearby state parks for free. Hot tip: grab dinner at the on-site restaurant — no driving needed after a long day.

Day Two

Big Sur, Fully

Thursday · July 2

  • 8:30 AM

    Pfeiffer Beach

    Arrive before 9am — parking fills fast in summer. Purple sand, a natural sea arch, and dramatic waves. $15 entry. Pure magic.

  • 11:30 AM

    Nepenthe Restaurant

    Perched 800ft above the ocean — one of the most iconic views in California. Great vegetarian options. Get a window table.

  • 1:30 PM

    McWay Falls

    An 80ft waterfall dropping straight onto a secluded beach. Short walk from parking. One of the most photographed spots in California — you'll see why.

  • 4:00 PM

    Afternoon Hike or Rest

    Partington Cove trail is stunning and less crowded. Or head back to Ventana for the hot pools before golden hour.

  • 7:00 PM

    Dinner at Big Sur Lodge Restaurant

    On-site restaurant with vegetarian-friendly options. Dine among the redwoods as the sun sets. Night two in Big Sur.

Day Three

North to Monterey, South to SLO

Friday · July 3

  • 9:00 AM

    Check out · Head to Carmel-by-the-Sea

    Just 45 minutes north. Wander the charming streets, pop into galleries, and grab brunch at Carmel Belle.

  • 11:00 AM

    Cannery Row, Monterey

    Walk the waterfront. The Monterey Bay Aquarium is right here if you want to pop in — it's one of the best in the world.

  • 1:00 PM

    Drive South toward SLO

    The stretch from Carmel back down through Big Sur on Hwy 1 heading south is jaw-dropping. Take it slow.

  • 4:30 PM

    Check in · Apple Farm Inn, San Luis Obispo

    Victorian farmhouse charm, pool, complimentary apple cider and cookies. Walking distance to downtown. One of the best value stays on the whole trip.

  • 7:00 PM

    Dinner in Downtown SLO

    Higuera Street is the heart of SLO's dining scene — lively, walkable, and great for July 3rd pre-celebration energy.

Day Four

4th of July · Santa Barbara

Saturday · July 4 🎆

  • 9:00 AM

    Breakfast at Petit Soleil

    Included with the stay. Multiple guests have called it the highlight of their trip. Enjoy it slowly before checking out.

  • 10:30 AM

    Morning stroll in Downtown SLO

    Farmers market, Higuera Street, local coffee shops. SLO has one of the best walking downtowns in California.

  • 1:00 PM

    Drive to Santa Barbara (~1.5 hrs)

    The 101 south through wine country is beautiful. Stop at Pismo Beach for 20 minutes if time allows.

  • 3:00 PM

    Check in · Harbor View Inn, Santa Barbara

    Across from the beach, near Stearns Wharf. Ask for an ocean-view room — you'll want it for the fireworks. Best value beachside option for July 4th weekend.

  • 6:00 PM

    Stearns Wharf & Sunset Walk

    The wharf is magical at sunset. Watch the sky turn pink and orange over the Pacific — peak California.

  • 9:00 PM

    4th of July Fireworks 🎆

    Santa Barbara's fireworks launch over the ocean. Watch from your balcony or head to the beach. One of the best places on the West Coast to spend the 4th.

Day Five

PCH Home

Sunday · July 6

  • 9:00 AM

    Breakfast in Santa Barbara

    Last slow morning. Walk Stearns Wharf one more time. Grab coffee and something light before hitting the road.

  • 10:30 AM

    Depart via Pacific Coast Highway

    Take PCH/Hwy 1 instead of the 101 or 405. It adds time but the drive through Ventura, Oxnard, and into Malibu is stunning.

  • 12:30 PM

    Malibu Farm at the Pier · Lunch

    Right on the Malibu Pier. Locally sourced, great vegetarian options, ocean views from every seat. The perfect final meal.

  • 2:30 PM

    Continue down PCH through LA

    Pacific Palisades, Santa Monica, Redondo Beach. Stop wherever you like — you're almost home.

  • 4:00 PM

    Newport Beach 🏠

    Home. With a lifetime of memories and probably about 3,000 new photos on your camera roll.

Day 5 · The Return

The Pacific Coast Highway Home

Instead of the freeway, take PCH all the way home. It adds about 45 minutes but you'll drive one of the most beautiful stretches of coastline in the world.

  • Santa Barbara → Ventura (Hwy 1 / PCH)

    Exit the 101 and pick up PCH heading south through Carpinteria and into Ventura. The Channel Islands come into view on clear days.

  • Ventura → Malibu

    This is the classic Malibu stretch — open bluffs, surfers below, the Santa Monica Mountains rising to your left. Windows down, music up.

  • Lunch at Malibu Farm · Malibu Pier

    Pull off at the Malibu Pier for your final road trip meal. Right on the ocean. Great vegetarian options and views to match.

  • Malibu → Pacific Palisades → Santa Monica

    PCH through the Palisades and down into Santa Monica. Walk the pier if you have the energy — or just keep rolling.

  • Santa Monica → Redondo Beach → Newport Beach

    PCH hugs the coast through Hermosa, Manhattan, and Redondo Beach all the way back to Newport. Welcome home. 🏠

Good to Know

🚗

Rental Car

Book via Costco Travel or Autoslash for best rates. Enterprise and National tend to be solid for longer road trips. Book in advance — July 4th weekend demand is high.

⛽

Fill up before Big Sur

Gas in Big Sur runs $8–10/gallon in summer. Fill up in Carmel or SLO. Don't risk it.

🅿️

Pfeiffer Beach Parking

Arrives by 9am in summer. The lot is small and fills by 10am on weekends. $15 entry fee.

📱

No Service in Big Sur

Cell service is virtually nonexistent. Download offline maps on Google Maps before you arrive.

🏨

Book Everything Now

July 4th weekend is peak season. The SB Hilton especially will sell out. Book all hotels ASAP.

🌫️

June Gloom / Morning Fog

July mornings can be foggy along the coast. It typically burns off by 11am — don't panic if it's grey in the AM.

🎒

Layer Up

Big Sur evenings can drop into the 50s even in July. Bring a jacket — and yes, Parth will carry the bags. 😊
